# LEGALFLOW — Future Go-to-Market Sprints

## Product
LEGALFLOW is an autonomous corporate intelligence agent
supporting preliminary M&A due diligence through verified
company records, financial analysis, and legal research.

## Sprint 1 — Legal Professional Validation

Goal: Validate the usefulness of LEGALFLOW reports.
Audience: 3–5 corporate and M&A lawyers.
Channel: Direct outreach and professional network.
Deliverable: Prototype demonstrations and sample reports.
Success metric: At least 4 of 5 reviewers consider
the reports useful for preliminary due diligence.

## Sprint 2 — Law Firm Pilot

Goal: Measure efficiency improvements.
Audience: 1–2 small or mid-sized corporate law firms.
Channel: Free pilot with anonymized or public company data.
Deliverable: Working research and reporting workflow.
Success metric: At least 40% reduction in preliminary
research time compared with the existing process.

## Sprint 3 — Commercial Validation

Goal: Evaluate willingness to pay.
Audience: Corporate law firms and legal operations teams.
Channel: Direct demonstrations and pilot referrals.
Deliverable: Pricing proposal and improved product prototype.
Success metric: Two organizations agree to a paid pilot.

## MVP vs Future Product

The five-day MVP demonstrates company research,
source validation, AI analysis, and report generation.

Production authentication, commercial deployment,
expanded jurisdiction support, and paid subscriptions
are outside the current MVP scope.

All success metrics are future targets, not achieved results.
