# Proposed future GTM sprints

These are plans, not completed experiments or measured business results.

## Sprint 1 — Small-firm pilot

- **Goal:** Check whether company briefings reduce preparation time and surface useful meeting questions.
- **Target buyer:** Managing partners and corporate/commercial lawyers at small German law firms.
- **Acquisition channel:** Personal outreach through local bar, legal-tech and founder networks; offer a short supervised pilot.
- **Deliverable:** A four-week pilot with 3–5 firms, onboarding guide, sample reports, and structured user feedback.
- **Success metric:** At least 10 briefings reviewed by lawyers; at least 70% rated useful or very useful; record preparation time before/after for each reviewed briefing.

## Sprint 2 — Reliability and workflow fit

- **Goal:** Improve source relevance, freshness display, and report usefulness based on pilot feedback.
- **Target buyer:** Pilot firms and adjacent corporate practice groups.
- **Acquisition channel:** Follow-up interviews, referrals from pilot users, and a focused webinar/demo for corporate lawyers.
- **Deliverable:** Revised source ranking, clearer evidence labels, and an export format shaped by lawyer feedback.
- **Success metric:** At least 20 lawyer-reviewed reports; 90% of factual passages linked to an accepted source in the review sample; fewer than 10% of reports rejected for identity mismatch or irrelevant coverage.

## Sprint 3 — Market testing and commercialization

- **Goal:** Test willingness to pay, procurement constraints and repeat usage before deciding whether to productize.
- **Target buyer:** German law firms with recurring corporate-client onboarding or counterparty research needs.
- **Acquisition channel:** Direct interviews, legal-tech events, and a limited paid-design-partner offer if usage and provider costs are understood.
- **Deliverable:** Pricing hypotheses, privacy/security review, provider-cost model, and a go/no-go commercialization memo.
- **Success metric:** 15 buyer interviews, at least 5 firms requesting continued use, and a documented gross-margin estimate based on real provider usage rather than assumed pricing.
